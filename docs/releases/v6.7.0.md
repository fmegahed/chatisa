# ChatISA v6.7.0 Release Notes

**Release Date:** September 24, 2026

---

## Overview

Job Scout and the Portfolio Builder now cover the whole Farmer School of
Business, not only ISA. Students pick their majors, co-majors and minor,
then mark courses Done, Taking now or Not yet in the groups the Miami
Bulletin uses. The course catalog, its skills and its links are refreshed
three times a year by a GitHub Action that opens a pull request for review;
nothing reaches students until that pull request is merged.

The professor reviewed and accepted the skill list, the course links, the
job tagger check and the prerequisite rules before release
(`docs/development/2026-09-24-catalog-review.md`).

## What students get

- **A major-first course checklist** in Job Scout's profile and the
  Portfolio Builder's Classes step. The FSB business core, the nine BSB
  majors, the Business Leadership and Entrepreneurship co-majors, and the
  AI for Business minor, grouped as in the 2026-27 Bulletin. Search covers
  all 166 courses, including earlier titles, so a renamed course is still
  found by the name students know.
- **Prerequisites come in automatically.** Marking ISA 401 "Taking now" as
  a Business Analytics major adds ISA 345, ISA 235 and CSE 148 as Done,
  labelled "Added because you are taking ISA 401", each with a Remove
  button. A removed course never comes back. "Or" choices follow the
  student's own path (their program, then the business core, then their
  department). Nothing is added for courses whose prerequisite allows
  instructor permission, a placement score, or a choice among four or more
  courses, or whose wording cannot be read with confidence.
- **"I've finished the required core courses"** marks the 18 courses every
  FSB student takes; the math course and capstone stay the student's pick.
- **A next-term prompt.** A course still marked "Taking now" from an
  earlier term is asked about once, above every Job Scout tab: Finished or
  Still taking.
- **"(in progress)" on portfolio pages.** Courses marked Taking now are
  labelled on the published page, and the model is told not to describe
  them as finished.
- **The showcase picker** offers the student's own Job Scout courses first,
  then search across every FSB course.

## Skills without overselling

- **30 business skills** (accounting, finance, economics, marketing,
  operations, HR, entrepreneurship, law, ethics, strategy and more). A job
  posting is tagged with one only when it names it in whole words.
- **Course level caps depth (professor's rule):** 100-level courses give
  exposure, 200-level at most applied, anchors start at 300; Excel in
  CSE 148 is the one exception. A student with only the required core now
  reads Strong in 3 skills, Working in 29 and Introduced in 27.
- **Guards in matching:** with no anchor a skill tops out at Working, and
  exposure alone at Introduced. Taking-now courses count at half weight.
  The student's own override always wins.
- **Two-model mapping:** a course link needs both Claude Sonnet 5 and
  GPT-6 Sol to agree, at the lower of their two levels. Approved links are
  never removed or re-levelled by a model run.

## Catalog refresh Action

`.github/workflows/catalog-refresh.yml` runs Feb 1, Jun 1 and Sep 1 and on
demand. It collects the Bulletin and the public syllabus library, maps only
the courses whose text changed (spend-capped at $10 a run by default), and
opens a pull request whose description lists every new anchor with its
evidence, level clashes, failed courses, and decisions still open from
earlier runs. The next syllabus pull is February 1, 2027.

## Operator notes

- Copy `web/deploy/chatisa-app.zip` to the server as usual (it is a tar;
  extract with 7-Zip or `tar -xf`). No server configuration changes.
- Repository setup is done: `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_API_KEY` are
  Actions secrets, and Actions may create pull requests. A first manual run
  from the Actions tab should end with "The catalog is unchanged; no pull
  request" at no cost.
- Job Scout profiles saved before this release migrate on first load (every
  course becomes Done); nothing is lost.

## Test and gate results

- Unit: 1,070 of 1,071 pass; the one failure is the known health-probe
  timeout under full-suite load, which passes alone. The catalog, Job Scout
  and portfolio suites pass 332/332 after the level-cap change.
- End to end: the full suite passes on desktop and mobile-320, including
  the new checklist spec (accessibility scans at 1280 and 320 px, no
  sideways scroll). Three load-sensitive tests (statsforecast in the
  sandbox, one Project Assistant test, one Ask Anything test) time out only
  under parallel load and pass alone; statsforecast behaves the same on
  v6.6.1.
- An independent whole-branch review found a prerequisite-parsing error
  that would have credited courses a student never took, plus eight other
  issues; all were fixed with failing-first tests. Two low-impact items are
  deferred: answering the next-term prompt clears unconfirmed resume
  suggestions in My Profile, and two lint warnings in an evaluation script.
